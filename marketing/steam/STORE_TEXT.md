## Short description (max 300 characters)
A lone knight descends into a drowned cathedral. Read every tell, parry the blow, punish the opening. Clash of Thrones is a 2D dark-fantasy action platformer with weighty combat, gothic pixel art, and a hard-won path through crumbling halls to the Drowned Abbot.

## About this game
**Every swing has weight.**
Clash of Thrones is a 2D side-scrolling action platformer set in a sunken, candle-lit cathedral. Learn your enemies, time your parries, and earn every step forward.

**Deliberate combat**
- Chain light attacks, charge heavy blows, and break guards
- Block, or parry at the last moment to stagger foes and finish them with an execution
- Unleash **Wrath** when the battle turns against you
- Manage stamina and a handful of healing flasks

**A knight who can climb**
Sprint, double jump, wall jump, grab ledges, climb ladders, dash through the air and slam down from above.

**A cathedral that fights back**
Crumbling ledges, swinging blades, fire jets and spike pits guard hidden passages and forgotten relics.

**Souls-like progression**
Collect ashes from the fallen and spend them at altars to grow stronger. Die, and your ashes stay where you fell - reach them again before they are lost.

**The Drowned Abbot**
A towering two-phase boss waits at the bottom of the nave.

## Demo description
The free demo contains the complete first area, *The Sunken Nave*: 7 zones, 4 enemy types, secrets, relics and the Drowned Abbot boss. Your feedback shapes the full game.
