CLASH OF THRONES - DEMO (pre-alpha)

A 2D dark-fantasy action platformer. This demo contains one full area,
The Sunken Nave, with its boss.

- It is an early test build: expect rough edges.
- Saves and settings are kept in your user folder and are NOT removed when
  you uninstall (delete %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Clash of Thrones to reset).
- Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: the demo is not code-signed yet.
